Rules of War

  • 10 waves, 45s build phase · castles have 3500 HP
  • Build towers and walls in your half to defend
  • Buy armies and bosses to attack your rival
  • +250 gold between waves, +10% interest on savings (max 100)
  • Place a tower on your own tower to upgrade it (max Lv3)
  • Arrows show each army's route during the build phase
  • Enemies can break walls · Esc pauses · M mutes
